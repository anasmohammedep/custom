Sonamarg, Kashmir

Some places feel like a dream, and Sonamarg was one of them. Snow-covered mountains, endless white landscapes, freezing winter air, and a kind of beauty that makes you forget everything else for a while. Every view felt magical, and the horse ride through those breathtaking surroundings made the experience even more special. It was one of those moments where the journey itself becomes a memory you wish you could relive again and again.

I may not have captured every little moment through my camera, but I know I lived some beautiful ones. Sometimes, the best memories aren’t the ones we record, but the ones we carry in our hearts.

Sonamarg wasn’t just a destination; it was a feeling, an experience, and a little piece of magic I’ll always look back on with a smile.

Dal Lake, Kashmir

Dal Lake, Kashmir — A Memory to Remember

One of the most beautiful memories from my college days was visiting Dal Lake in Kashmir. Surrounded by majestic mountains, crystal-clear waters, and the peaceful beauty of nature, Dal Lake felt like a little piece of heaven on Earth.

The shikara rides across the lake, the charming houseboats, the cool breeze, and the breathtaking views made every moment unforgettable. There was something magical about being there — a kind of peace and beauty that pictures can capture, but words can never fully explain.

It wasn't just a place I visited; it was an experience I’ll always cherish. A beautiful chapter of my college life, captured in photos and videos, and kept alive in my memories forever. ❤️

Taj Mahal

Symbol of Love

Taj Mahal — Symbol of Eternal Love

Visiting the Taj Mahal during my college trip was an unforgettable experience. Seeing its magnificent white marble architecture and beautiful surroundings in person was truly amazing.

Located in Agra, India, the Taj Mahal was built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal. It is known worldwide as a symbol of eternal love and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture.

Exploring this historic wonder with my college friends made the trip even more special. It was a beautiful experience filled with memories, history, and the magic of a place that celebrates love forever.
